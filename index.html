Cultural Heritage

Cultural Heritage

Sumy Region

Krolevets Weaving

In Krolevets, people weave red and white towels with special patterns. These woven towels are called rushnyky.

Pottery

Ukrainian potters make jugs, bowls and pots from clay. They often paint them with bright patterns.

Pysanka

People decorate Easter eggs with wax and dyes. Every pattern has its own meaning.

Historic Places and Events

Historic Places and Events

From Konotop to Baturyn

Konotop · Sumy Region

The Battle of Konotop, 1659

In 1659, the Cossack army of Hetman Ivan Vyhovsky and their Crimean Tatar allies defeated the army of the Muscovite tsar near Konotop.

Baturyn · Chernihiv Region

Baturyn Citadel

Baturyn was the hetmans’ capital. In 1708, Peter I’s troops destroyed the town and killed thousands of people (estimates: about 9,000–15,000). Today’s citadel is a reconstruction.

Baturyn · Chernihiv Region

Kyrylo Rozumovsky’s Palace

This is the palace of Kyrylo Rozumovsky, the last hetman of Cossack Ukraine. It was built in 1799–1803 to a design by the Scottish architect Charles Cameron.

Local and National Traditions

Local and National Traditions

Christmas Carol Singing

At Christmas, carol singers go from house to house with a star and sing traditional songs.

Korovai

Korovai is a traditional Ukrainian decorated bread, a symbol of hospitality and celebration.

Green Holidays — Pentecost

At Pentecost, people decorate their homes with fresh green branches and maple leaves. They also put fragrant grass on the floor.

Ukrainian Folk Heritage

Ukrainian Folk Heritage

Once Upon a Dog

A Soviet cartoon (Soyuzmultfilm, 1982) by Eduard Nazarov. It is based on the Ukrainian folk tale ‘Sirko’, and we hear Ukrainian folk songs in it.

Mavka

Mavka is a forest spirit from Ukrainian folklore. She is the heroine of ‘The Forest Song’ by Lesya Ukrainka.

Hopak

Hopak is a lively Ukrainian dance with high jumps. The Virsky Ensemble performs it in national costumes.

Education in Ukraine

Education in Ukraine

Primary School

Children usually start school at six. At the First Bell and the Last Bell, a senior student often carries a first-year pupil, who rings a bell.

Secondary School

At the end of secondary school, graduates celebrate. They often dance a waltz for their families and teachers.

Sumy

University

After school, many students go to university. This is Sumy State University.

Music and Musical Instruments

Music and Musical Instruments

Shchedryk

Shchedryk is a traditional Ukrainian New Year’s song. Its melody became famous around the world as ‘Carol of the Bells’.

The Bandura

The bandura is a traditional Ukrainian string instrument. Musicians pluck the strings with their fingers.

Lvivyanky Bandura Ensemble — Pro ridnyi Lviv (About Our Native Lviv).
